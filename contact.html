Questions we hear a lot

Do I need to know coding already?

No. We run sessions for complete beginners and for people who already build things. You learn by making projects with other members, and nobody expects you to arrive knowing everything.

Is the club free?

Yes. Joining and attending meetups costs nothing. Just show up on Saturday and bring a laptop if you have one.

Can first years join?

Yes. First years are welcome, and it's one of the best times to start. Pick the "Join the club" topic in the form above or come to the next meetup.
